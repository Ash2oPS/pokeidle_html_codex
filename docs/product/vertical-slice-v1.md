# Vertical Slice V1

## Scope

- 2 towns
- 2 combat zones
- progression order: town 1 -> combat zone 1 -> combat zone 2 -> town 2
- town 2 contains the first gym battle
- 2 main quests in sequence
- 1 side quest

## Combat Content

- slot-based idle combat
- one enemy at the center
- up to six team slots
- empty slots still consume time
- typing and reaction rules are active
- talents exist but broad talent coverage is not required in V1

## Tool Scope

- zone editor
- Pokemon viewer
- dialogue editor

## Delivery Intent

- ship one vertical slice that proves the architecture, the save pipeline, the responsive UI shell, and the authoring workflow
- defer broad content volume and deep balance passes until the slice is stable
